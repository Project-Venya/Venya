# 🪄 Project Venya

> Your conversational personal assistant, built with Rust and open-source AI models.

---

## ✨ About Venya

Venya is a voice-first, AI-powered personal assistant designed to help you stay organized, reflect on your day, learn continuously, and manage your personal and professional life — all through natural conversations.

We're building Venya in public, sharing every milestone from the first line of code to a fully working assistant!

---

## 🚀 Goals

- Understand and manage your weekly and daily schedules.
- Chat with you each morning to plan your day.
- Remind you of tasks throughout your day.
- Help you reflect and adjust your goals at the end of each day.
- Read books aloud during scheduled sessions and discuss key insights.
- Serve as your personal and professional journal assistant.
- Adapt and evolve with your needs over time.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Rust:** Blazing fast, safe, and reliable.
- **Dioxus:** For building a beautiful, cross-platform UI (inspired by React and Vue.js).
- **TailwindCSS:** For minimal, clean design styling.
- **Open-Source AI Models:** For Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) capabilities.
- **SQLite + Cloud APIs:** To manage and store your data efficiently.
